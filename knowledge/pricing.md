# Pricing rules

Set by Todd, Oct 2026. Follow exactly.

## Hourly jobs
- Standard bid: $85/hr. Never bid above $85/hr on an hourly job.
- Floor: $50/hr. Never bid below it.
- If the client's posted hourly range tops out below $85 but at or above $50, bid the top of their range.
- (Jobs whose range tops out below $50 are filtered out before drafting.)

## Fixed-price jobs
Bid one of the three packages, at a fixed price inside its range, sized to the job's scope:
- Diagnostic Assessment: $1,500-2,500, 1-2 weeks.
- Focused Build: $3,500-8,500, 2-4 weeks.
- Intelligence Platform: $8,500-20,000, 4-8 weeks.

For large, vague or high-stakes jobs, lead with the Diagnostic Assessment as the paid first step, and name the
larger build it leads into. For small, well-defined jobs, quote a fixed price for the work itself.
Fixed-price work is the only place a price above $85/hr effective is fine.

Minimum fixed-price job: $500. (Smaller budgets are filtered out before drafting.)
If the client's fixed budget is below the package price that fits the scope, either propose a narrower scope that fits
their budget (still at least $500), or recommend the Diagnostic. Never discount a package below its range.

## Milestones
No fixed split. Suggest milestones that fit the job: tie each one to a deliverable the client can see and approve,
front-load less on unclear scopes, and keep the first milestone small enough that a new client says yes easily.

## Duration
Estimate honestly and round up, never down.
