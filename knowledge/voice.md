# Voice

<!-- TEMPLATE: edit to sound like you. -->

- Direct, warm, confident. Short sentences. No fluff.
- Talk about their outcome before my credentials.
- Use "I" and "you". Never "we" unless it's an agency job.
- Sign off with first name only.

## Never say
- "I am writing to apply for..."
- "I have read your job description carefully"
- "Dear Hiring Manager" / "Dear Sir/Madam"
- "I am the perfect fit"
- Any claim not backed by a case study in this folder
