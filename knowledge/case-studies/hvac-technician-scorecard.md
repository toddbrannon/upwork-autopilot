# Field Service Performance System

- Client type: HVAC company running on ServiceTitan data
- Problem: no clear view of individual technician performance or where money was being lost
- What I did: built a no-VBA technician scorecard tracking revenue per tech, callback rates, productivity metrics and
  profit estimates; an automated Insights tab surfaces top performers and quantifies losses against configurable targets
- Result: technician performance and losses against target are visible automatically. No verified figure: describe what the system does; never state a number for this project.
